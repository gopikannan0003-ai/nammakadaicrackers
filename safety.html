SAFETY FIRST

Celebrate Responsibly

Enjoy festive celebrations responsibly. Always follow the instructions provided with each product and make safety a priority.

📖

Follow Instructions

Read and follow the safety instructions printed on the product packaging before use.

👨‍👩‍👧

Adult Supervision

Children should never handle fireworks without close supervision from a responsible adult.

🏠

Choose a Safe Area

Use only in an appropriate open area away from buildings, vehicles and other hazards.

💧

Keep Water Nearby

Keep water or an appropriate fire-safety resource nearby.

🚫

Never Re-light

Never try to relight or handle a product that did not function as expected.

👕

Suitable Clothing

Choose comfortable clothing and keep a safe distance during celebrations.

🛡️ Important Safety Reminders

✓ Always follow the product label and local rules.
✓ Keep spectators at a safe distance.
✓ Keep children supervised by responsible adults.
✓ Keep pets safely indoors during celebrations.
✓ Do not use fireworks indoors.
✓ Do not carry fireworks in pockets or clothing.
✓ Never attempt to modify or combine products.
✓ Dispose of used materials only when completely safe.
